Humanized Rudolph:
Most of what they say about Santa Claus is true. Every winter he brings gifts to those who behaved well all year.
And not only to children! Grown-up heroes who fought hard all year get gifts too. I can tell you've slain plenty of monsters, so I'm sure he has something for you.
Each time he visits, he waits somewhere new, out in the hunting grounds of Aden. Beside him stands a Christmas tree that blesses everyone nearby.
